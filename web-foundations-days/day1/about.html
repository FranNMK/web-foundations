About Quick Notes

Quick Notes is a simple note-taking application built to help you capture ideas, reminders, and thoughts on the go. Whether it's a brilliant idea, a to-do list item, or a simple note, Quick Notes helps you remember what matters.

Features include:

  • Adding new notes
  • Viewing all your notes
  • Counting your total notes
